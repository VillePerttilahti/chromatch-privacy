# Privacy Policy — Chromatch

**Last updated: May 2026**

---

## 1. Overview

Chromatch ("the app") is a color memory game developed by Ville Perttilahti. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

By downloading or using Chromatch, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data stored on your device
Chromatch stores the following data **locally on your device only**:
- Game history (your last 3 game results)
- Selected difficulty level

This data is stored using Apple's UserDefaults and **never transmitted to any server or third party**.

### 2.2 Data we do NOT collect
Chromatch does not collect:
- Your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
- Location data
- Health or fitness data
- Contacts, photos, or files
- Device identifiers for tracking purposes
- Any data from children under the age of 13

---

## 3. Advertising

Chromatch may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain data to serve ads, including:
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS)
- Approximate location (country/region level)
- Device information (device type, operating system)
- Ad interaction data

You can limit ad tracking at any time in your iPhone settings under **Privacy & Security → Tracking**.

Chromatch does not use personalized advertising directed at children. AdMob is configured to serve non-personalized ads in contexts where child-directed treatment is required.

For more information on how Google handles data in AdMob, see Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## 4. In-App Purchases

Chromatch offers an optional one-time purchase to remove advertisements (€1.99). This transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Chromatch does not process or store any payment information.

For information on how Apple handles purchase data, see Apple's Privacy Policy:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Chromatch is suitable for all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. No account registration, profile creation, or personal data submission is required or possible within the app.

If you believe your child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will promptly address the concern.

---

## 6. Data Sharing

Chromatch does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to third parties, except as described in Section 3 (Advertising) regarding AdMob.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can delete all app data at any time by:
- Deleting the app from your device
- Going to **Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Chromatch → Delete App**

---

## 8. App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

In accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework (iOS 14.5+), Chromatch will request your permission before tracking your activity across other companies' apps and websites for advertising purposes. You may deny this permission and the app will continue to function normally with non-personalized ads.
